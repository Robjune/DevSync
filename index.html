ABOUT OUR PROJECT

What is DevSync?

DevSync is a collaborative developer portfolio created by CPE-201 Group 3. It serves as a central platform where our team can showcase our skills, individual profiles, projects, and development experience.

Our Goal

To create a responsive and organized portfolio that represents our team's abilities and progress in web development.

Our Skills

We apply HTML, CSS, JavaScript, interface design, testing, documentation, and collaborative project development.

Our Team

Six Computer Engineering students working together through development, design, content creation, testing, and documentation.
